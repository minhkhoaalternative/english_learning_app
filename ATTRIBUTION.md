## Scope of credit and level labels

Earlier B1–C1 expansion lessons without a sources field and all existing vocabulary entries are independently authored material that has not been fully checked item by item against these references. They are not attributed to British Council or Cambridge. New source metadata records exactly which lessons were checked.

B1/B2/C1 are instructional target levels, not an official CEFR certification or an exhaustive syllabus. British Council groups many intermediate topics as B1–B2; this app chooses a suggested study order. Cambridge reference material is not treated as a CEFR classification. Existing approximate British English IPA is not an authoritative pronunciation dictionary.
